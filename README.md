## About Me

I’m a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science with a strong focus on robotics, perception, computer vision, and autonomous systems. My work centers on ROS 2, RGB-D perception, SLAM, autonomous navigation, and PX4-based drone autonomy.

I have hands-on experience in Gazebo simulation, Nav2, point-cloud processing, semantic mapping, and real-time vision pipelines for mobile robots and autonomous machines.
